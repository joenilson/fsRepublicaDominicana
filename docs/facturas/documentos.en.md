# Supported documents

The plugin adds the DGII tax data to the purchase and sales documents of FacturaScripts. The screenshots on this page
come from the documents generated by the [release test suite](../desarrollo/pruebas-lanzamiento.md) on every version,
so they show what the plugin does today.

## Summary

| Document | Purchase | Sale | Plugin tax data |
|----------|:--------:|:----:|-----------------|
| Supplier / customer | ✅ | ✅ | RNC or ID number, default receipt type, default withholding |
| Product | ✅ | ✅ | Service or goods (606 classification), additional taxes |
| Order | ✅ | ✅ | No NCF: not a tax document |
| Delivery note | ✅ | ✅ | No NCF: not a tax document |
| Invoice | ✅ | ✅ | NCF or e-CF, receipt type, payment type and movement type |
| Credit note (04 / 34) | ✅ | ✅ | Negative amount, cap on the original invoice |
| Debit note (03 / 33) | ✅ | ✅ | Positive amount, original invoice |
| Cancelled invoice | — | ✅ | Cancellation type; reported in the 608, not in the 607 |
| Electronic receipt (e-CF) | — | ✅ | With the FeRepublicaDominicana plugin: signed XML and QR code |

Supported receipt types: 01 tax credit, 02 consumer, 03 debit note, 04 credit note, 14 special regimes and 15
government, and e-CF 31 to 47. See [receipt types](../referencia/tipos-comprobante.md).

## Purchases

### Supplier

![Supplier record](../assets/img/documentos/compra-proveedor.png)

The supplier stores its RNC or ID number and the withholding applied to its invoices. The 606 is filled from them.

### Purchase order and delivery note

![Purchase order](../assets/img/documentos/compra-pedido.png)

The order is the first step and has no NCF. It becomes a delivery note with the status button, and the delivery note
becomes an invoice.

![Purchase delivery note](../assets/img/documentos/compra-albaran.png)

### Purchase invoice in pesos

![Purchase invoice in Dominican pesos](../assets/img/documentos/compra-factura-dop.png)

Invoice with one goods line and one service line, with 18 % ITBIS. The supplier's NCF is typed on the invoice and the
plugin warns if it was already registered. The 606 splits the amount into services and goods by product.

### Purchase invoice in dollars

![Purchase invoice in dollars](../assets/img/documentos/compra-factura-usd.png)

In another currency the invoice is saved in that currency, with its exchange rate, and still carries an NCF.

### Purchase credit note

![Purchase credit note](../assets/img/documentos/compra-nota-credito.png)

Created from the **Returns** tab of the invoice. It is a rectifying invoice with type 04 (or 34 if electronic) and
negative amounts. In the 606 it is reported as positive, with the NCF it modifies.

### Purchase debit note

![Purchase debit note](../assets/img/documentos/compra-nota-debito.png)

Extra charge from a supplier on an earlier invoice: type 03 (or 33), positive amount and the invoice it modifies.

### Purchase with additional taxes

![Purchase invoice with ISC and CDT](../assets/img/documentos/compra-impuestos-adicionales.png)

Each line can carry ISC, CDT, legal tip or first plate. Amounts are calculated with the catalog rate, or typed by hand
when the supplier's invoice prevails. See [additional taxes](../configuracion/impuestos.md).

### Purchase with ITBIS and ISR withholding

![Purchase invoice with 30 % ITBIS withholding](../assets/img/documentos/compra-retencion-itbis.png)

The ITBIS withholding (30 % or 100 %) is calculated on the invoice ITBIS and reported in the 606.

![Purchase invoice with ISR withholding on fees](../assets/img/documentos/compra-retencion-isr.png)

The ISR withholding (codes 1 to 9) is reported with its type in the 606. See [withholdings](../configuracion/retenciones.md).

## Sales

### Customers by receipt type

The customer's receipt type decides which NCF is proposed on their invoices.

![Consumer customer](../assets/img/documentos/venta-cliente-consumo.png)

**Consumer (02):** no RNC required.

![Customer with tax credit](../assets/img/documentos/venta-cliente-credito-fiscal.png)

**Tax credit (01):** requires an RNC or ID number; the plugin can look it up in the DGII database.

![Special regime customer](../assets/img/documentos/venta-cliente-regimen-especial.png)

**Special regimes (14):** for exempt customers, free zones and similar.

### Sales order and delivery note

![Sales order](../assets/img/documentos/venta-pedido.png)

![Sales delivery note](../assets/img/documentos/venta-albaran.png)

As in purchases: no NCF; the NCF is assigned when the invoice is generated.

### Consumer invoice (02)

![Consumer invoice](../assets/img/documentos/venta-factura-consumo.png)

When the invoice is approved the plugin takes the next B02 NCF of the active range.

### Tax credit invoice (01)

![Tax credit invoice](../assets/img/documentos/venta-factura-credito-fiscal.png)

It carries the B01 NCF and is reported in the 607 with the customer's RNC.

### Special regimes invoice (14)

![Special regimes invoice](../assets/img/documentos/venta-factura-regimen-especial.png)

### Invoices in dollars and euros

![Consumer invoice in dollars](../assets/img/documentos/venta-factura-usd.png)

![Consumer invoice in euros](../assets/img/documentos/venta-factura-eur.png)

It carries the same NCF type as in pesos; the currency and exchange rate are stored on the invoice.

### Sales credit note

![Sales credit note](../assets/img/documentos/venta-nota-credito.png)

Generated from **Returns**: type 04, B04 NCF and negative quantities. The plugin rejects a note with a positive amount
or one that, added to the others, exceeds the invoice. See [credit and debit notes](notas.md).

### Sales debit note

![Sales debit note](../assets/img/documentos/venta-nota-debito.png)

Extra charge on an invoice: type 03, B03 NCF and positive amount.

### Cancelled invoice

![Cancelled sales invoice](../assets/img/documentos/venta-factura-anulada.png)

The invoice stays in **Cancelled** status. It is not in the 607 and is in the 608 with its cancellation type.

### Sale with additional taxes

![Sales invoice with ISC and legal tip](../assets/img/documentos/venta-impuestos-adicionales.png)

Goods with ISC (cigarettes, specific plus *ad valorem*) and a service with the 10 % legal tip. The **Other taxes**
field in the footer adds up the additional taxes without mixing them with ITBIS.

## Electronic receipts (e-CF)

With the **FeRepublicaDominicana** plugin, invoices and notes can be electronic: types 31, 32, 33, 34 and so on, with
signed XML, signing date, security code and a QR code on the PDF.

![e-CF 31 sales invoice](../assets/img/factura-venta-ecf31.png)

![Electronic credit note 34](../assets/img/nota-credito-34.png)

![Electronic debit note 33](../assets/img/nota-debito-33.png)

See [electronic receipts](ecf.md).
